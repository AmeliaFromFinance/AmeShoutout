Streamer.bot extension

Shoutouts that show the clip

Shout out a streamer and one of their clips plays in OBS. Pronouns, raid counts, stream teams and Twitch's /shoutout are handled for you.

Get it Read the guide

Free. Needs Streamer.bot 1.0 or newer and OrchidUI. Windows, Linux and macOS.

More than a chat message

One action does it all, from a mod's !so to a raid landing mid-game.

Clips in OBS

A random clip from the streamer, or one you link. If several shoutouts land at once, the clips take turns.

Their pronouns

From pr.alejo.io, so the message says she, he, they or whatever they use.

Twitch /shoutout

Queued two minutes apart, as Twitch needs. Chat hears when one has to wait.

Auto shoutouts

For your list of people and your stream team, on their first message of the stream.

Custom messages

Give a streamer their own message with !scm. Tokens fill in their game, link and pronouns.

Raids and teams

Adds how many times they've raided you and the stream teams you share.

Try a message

Change the message or the streamer and see what chat would get. Same tokens as in the app.

  1. {LIVE_STATUS} becomes "she is currently streaming" or "she was last streaming".
  2. {SUBJECT} {OBJECT} {VERB} follow their pronouns.
  3. :lower :upper :title change the case, like {GAME:title}.

Set up in a few minutes

Everything lives in one action. The settings window opens after the import and saves as you go.

  1. Install OrchidUI. Put OrchidUI.dll next to Streamer.bot.exe, then unzip the host for your system into an OrchidUI folder beside it. Windows 10 or 11 orchid-host-win-x64.zip Linux (Wine, Bottles, Lutris, Proton) orchid-host-linux-x64.zip Linux on ARM orchid-host-linux-arm64.zip macOS on Apple silicon (Whisky, CrossOver, Wine) orchid-host-osx-arm64.zip On Linux and macOS, use that host even though Streamer.bot runs in Wine. Then run chmod +x OrchidUI/orchid-host, and on macOS also xattr -d com.apple.quarantine OrchidUI/orchid-host. More help
  2. Import. Copy the import string, then click Import in Streamer.bot and paste it.
  3. Turn on the commands. Right-click the [Ame] Shoutout group under Commands and click Enable All.
  4. Set up OBS. Click Set up OBS on the Clips tab, then add the AmeShoutout scene to your scenes.

Commands

Moderators can use these by default.

!so <user>Shoutout with a random clip
!so <user> noShoutout without a clip
!so <user> <clip link>Shoutout with that clip
!as add <user>Adds them to the auto shoutout list
!as remove <user>Takes them off the list
!scm <user> <message>Gives them their own shoutout message
!scm <user>Puts them back on the default message

!shoutout, !autoshoutout and !setcustommessage work too.
